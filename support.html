怎么验一台二手 Mac？/ How do I inspect a used Mac?

在要买的那台 Mac 上安装并打开 Mac-List：自动检测会马上开始；然后按「交互测试」逐项测键盘、屏幕、扬声器等；最后在「人工核对」里确认「查找我的 Mac」已关闭、卖家已退出 Apple 账户。「报告」页会给出结论，可以导出 PDF 或图片。
Install and open Mac-List on the Mac you are buying. The automatic checks start right away; then work through Hands-on Tests (keyboard, screen, speakers and more), and in Manual Checks confirm that Find My Mac is off and the seller has signed out of their Apple Account. The Report page gives the verdict and exports a PDF or image.

为什么不能自动检测激活锁？/ Why can't it detect Activation Lock automatically?

Mac App Store 的 App 运行在沙盒里，macOS 不允许沙盒 App 读取激活锁状态。所以 Mac-List 会一键带你打开对应的系统设置页面，告诉你看哪里，由你亲自确认。
Mac App Store apps run in a sandbox, and macOS does not let sandboxed apps read the Activation Lock status. Mac-List takes you straight to the right System Settings page and tells you what to look for, so you can confirm it yourself.

为什么没有 CPU 温度和风扇转速？/ Why no CPU temperature or fan speed?

同样是沙盒限制：读取温度传感器需要的接口不对 App Store 的 App 开放。Mac-List 改用系统的发热状态，并在满载测试中观察性能是否明显下降，来发现散热问题。
Also a sandbox limit: the temperature sensors are not available to App Store apps. Mac-List uses the system thermal state instead, and watches for a large slowdown during the load test to catch cooling problems.

蓝牙显示「未获得权限」/ Bluetooth says access is not allowed

到「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 蓝牙」里允许 Mac-List，然后在 App 里点「开始检测」重新检测。
Allow Mac-List in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Bluetooth, then click Run Checks in the app.

导出的报告里序列号为什么是打码的？/ Why is the serial number masked in reports?

为了保护卖家和买家的隐私，导出时默认只显示前 4 位和后 3 位。如果需要完整序列号，导出前勾选「导出时显示完整序列号」。
To protect privacy, exports show only the first 4 and last 3 characters by default. Tick "Show full serial number in export" before exporting if you need it in full.

验机记录保存在哪里？/ Where are saved inspections kept?

只保存在这台 Mac 上 App 自己的文件夹里，不会上传。在「历史记录」里右键可以删除。
Only on this Mac, in the app's own folder. Nothing is uploaded. Right-click an entry in History to delete it.
